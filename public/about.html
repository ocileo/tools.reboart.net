Our History

ToolBox was born from the need for an online tools platform that is truly free and without limitations. We realized that many users struggle to find adequate tools because of file size limits, daily usage caps, or subscription requirements.

Starting from a simple idea in 2024, ToolBox has grown into a platform with 35+ tools across various categories. We continue to add new tools based on user feedback and needs.

Today, ToolBox is used by thousands of users from various backgrounds, including students, freelancers, professionals, and business owners.
