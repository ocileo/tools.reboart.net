Privacy Policy - ToolBox

Privacy Policy
Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction

ToolBox ("We", "Us", "Our") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

By using ToolBox, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect data that is truly necessary to improve our services:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with ToolBox (pages visited, tools used).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device information.
  • Uploaded Files: Files you upload to our tools are processed only on the client-side (your browser) and are never stored on our servers.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your experience and analyze traffic.

Important: All processes at ToolBox run client-side (in your browser). Your files are NEVER sent to our servers. Your privacy is our top priority.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain ToolBox services
  • Improve and optimize the tools we offer
  • Analyze usage trends for feature development
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues
  • Send updates and important information about the service

4. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Client-Side Processing: All files are processed in your browser, not on our servers
  • HTTPS Encryption: All communication uses SSL/TLS encryption
  • No Storage: We do not store your files or personal data on our servers
  • Data Minimization: We only collect data that is truly necessary

5. Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences (language, theme, etc.)
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve performance and site speed

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings. You can also delete cookies at any time.

6. Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. However, we may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics to understand site usage
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Workers to provide the service

These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for data collection

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@tools.reboart.net.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: support@tools.reboart.net

Website: tools.reboart.net

Conclusion: ToolBox is committed to protecting your privacy. We do not store your files on servers, we do not share your data, and all processing happens in your browser. Your trust is our priority.
